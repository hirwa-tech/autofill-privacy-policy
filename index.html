Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoFill: Form & Table Assistant handles information when you use the Chrome extension.

1. What the Extension Does

AutoFill: Form & Table Assistant helps users fill forms and tables on webpages more efficiently. It can assist with repetitive data-entry tasks, including entering student marks where applicable.

2. Information Accessed

The extension may access content from the webpage where the user chooses to use the extension. This may include text, form fields, table contents, and other webpage elements necessary to provide its form and table assistance features.

The extension only accesses webpage information necessary for its functionality.

3. Personal Information

The extension does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

If personal information appears in a webpage that the user chooses to process, such information may be accessed as part of the webpage content required for the extension's functionality.

4. Student Information

When the extension is used with pages containing student information, such as student names or marks, that information may be accessed as part of the webpage content being processed by the user.

The extension does not sell student information or use it for advertising purposes.

5. Data Storage

The extension may use Chrome's local storage functionality to save extension settings and preferences.

Information stored locally by the extension is not sold to third parties.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties for advertising, marketing, or unrelated purposes.

7. Authentication Information

If authentication or activation functionality is added to the extension, authentication information will only be used for the purpose of providing and protecting the extension's functionality.

8. Payments

The current version of the extension does not require payment to use its functionality. If paid features are introduced in the future, payment processing will be handled according to the privacy policies of the payment provider used.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the extension. However, no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the AutoFill: Form & Table Assistant extension, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the extension's official support page.
